Key Insights

What makes us different?

We combine local ownership with global experience. Our consultants have 5–10+ years of international expertise, and we pride ourselves on transparency, integrity, and a personalised approach that supports both clients and candidates from the very start to finish.

Who are our main clients?

We work with corporate organisations across a wide spectrum, including government entities, media houses, hospitality groups, financial institutions, healthcare providers, real estate firms, and retail chains.

What sectors do we cover?

We cover a broad range of sectors, including Wholesale & Retail, Consulting Firms, Hotel & Catering, Fintech, Real Estate, Media & Entertainment, Information Technology (IT), and Finance & Banking.

How do we find candidates?

We use a multi-channel sourcing strategy that combines our extensive international network, targeted job advertising, database mining, referrals, and direct headhunting. We recruit from a wide range of countries, including the Philippines, India, Nepal, Sri Lanka, Turkey, Albania, Bangladesh, Uganda, Kenya, Uzbekistan, Indonesia, and Kyrgyzstan.

What is our recruitment process?

We follow a structured yet smooth process: creating job posts, profiling and shortlisting candidates, conducting employer interviews, performing reference checks, and finalising with a formal agreement — ensuring a seamless journey from selection to deployment.
